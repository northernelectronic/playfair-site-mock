How it works

Four steps, from theme to RSVP.

  1. 1

    Pick a theme

    Start with one of the example games, or tell us the occasion and the feeling you want. Holiday party, stag night, wedding weekend, team event, and more.

  2. 2

    We customize it

    Your names, date, place, host line, and RSVP questions go in. We can tweak the look so it feels like your event, not a generic template.

  3. 3

    Guests play

    You send one private link. Guests open it on their phone, learn the controls in a short how-to, and play a level that usually takes a minute or two. If they get stuck, they can get a hand or skip to the invite.

  4. 4

    The invite is revealed

    After they finish, the details appear with an RSVP. Family name, headcount, a dish, a cookie contest, a hotel room — whatever you need to collect.

FAQ

Questions hosts usually ask.

Does this work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Guests open a link in the phone’s browser. It’s built first for iPhone, and it also plays in Android browsers. There’s no App Store or Google Play download.

Do guests need to install an app?

No. The game is a web page. If someone wants it to feel like an app, they can use Add to Home Screen. That’s optional.

How does RSVP collection work?

The reveal screen can include the questions you care about — name, how many are coming, a dish, a plus-one, a hotel room, and so on. How those replies reach you (email, a form service, or a spreadsheet) would be set up when a real order is taken. This mock does not collect RSVPs.

How long does a custom game take?

Example only, not a promise: dressing an existing theme could be on the order of about a week, depending on the brief. A brand-new world takes longer. A real turnaround would be agreed in writing before work starts.

Are the adult games explicit?

No. The stag and bachelorette demos are cheeky cartoon party games, labelled Adults 19+. They are not explicit and are not for kids.
